Card titles rotate accent colors across the three themes (peach → honey → mahogany).

To change the palette, edit the seven role variables at the top of styles.css (--dark, --mid, --light, --accent-1 through --accent-4). The legacy --c-mahogany, --c-peach, etc. aliases also live in that block — they all point to the role variables, so the site updates from one place.
