What's in each convo

Convo Guide

Carefully crafted questions to guide a productive discussion without a facilitator.

Spark

A short video, classroom transcript, or piece of student work to ground the discussion.

Reference Lesson

Each spark is contextualized in a high-quality lesson that's mathematically rigorous and rooted in the real world.

Can I get the reference lessons for myself?

Yes. Every Spark is contextualized in a Citizen Math lesson — mathematically rigorous, conversational, and rooted in the real world. You don't need to teach the lesson to use the convo, but having it on hand gives every participant a shared anchor for the discussion.
